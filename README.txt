ANDROID CONTROLS
Automatic acceleration. Hold arrows to steer; hold Drift while steering.
Hold Boost for nitro; release it after emptying the reserve to recharge.
Dodge the marked lane when a rival rocket is incoming, or use a Shield.
Tap the item slot to use a pickup. Pause is top right.
Tap Boost when the countdown reaches 1 for a launch boost.
Pause settings include steering sensitivity, left-handed buttons and
Auto / High / Performance graphics. Preferences save on this browser.
Leaving fullscreen pauses the race; tap Resume to continue.
Charge blue, gold, then pink drift sparks; release Drift for a boost.
Jackpot Cup: finish all three races to save your best cup result. Leaving the
cup ends the current run. Points: 10/8/6/4/2/1; ties use the latest race result.

PC CONTROLS
Arrows or A/D: steer. Space: drift. Shift: boost. E: item. P/Escape: pause.
